## 🎯 **What We Do**

MykoVolt develops **biodegradable fungal bio-batteries** that power disposable IoT sensors. Our technology enables **zero-waste sensor networks** that operate underground, in darkness, and **compost away at end-of-life** — eliminating e-waste and compliance headaches.

```
Traditional batteries:  Leave behind toxic waste  →  Must be retrieved
MykoVolt batteries:      Naturally decompose      →  No retrieval needed
```

---

## 🔬 **The Technology**

### How It Works
1. **Fungi consume sugar** → Release electrons through metabolism
2. **Electrons flow** through conductive carbon network → Generate power
3. **Power regional sensors** → Collect data (7-14 days)
4. **Fungi continue growing** → Decompose battery + housing (90 days)

### Key Metrics
| Spec | Current | Target (2027) | Target (2028) |
|------|---------|---------------|---------------|
| Power density | 12.5 µW/cm² | 50 µW/cm² | 200 µW/cm² |
| Lifetime | 7-14 days | 14-21 days | 30+ days |
| Cost/unit | €5-10 | €0.50 | €0.15 |
| Biodegradability | 90% | 95% | 99% |
| Shelf life | 6 months | 12 months | 18 months |
