## 🎙️ **Speaker Notes: Key Messages**

### Opening Hook (Slide 1):
> "What if I told you the answer to e-waste is growing in your garden? Fungi that eat sugar and generate electricity. It sounds like science fiction, but it's peer-reviewed science from the Swiss Federal Labs. We've curated 90+ papers, and now we're turning it into a business."

### The Problem (Slide 2):
> "Today, deploying soil sensors means either: Using solar panels that don't work underground, Li-ion batteries that must be retrieved, or accepting that 50% will fail prematurely. The EU has had enough — starting in 2026, they're mandating retrieval of industrial batteries. But what if you could just... leave them?"

### The Science (Slide 3):
> "We use two fungi: Trametes produces laccase enzymes that capture electrons, and Saccharomyces (brewer's yeast) donates electrons through its metabolism. Together, they create a living battery that generates 50-200 microwatts per square centimeter — enough to power a soil sensor for 7-14 days."

### The Solution (Slide 4):
> "Our value proposition is simple: Drop it. Monitor it. Forget it. No retrieval, no waste, no compliance headaches. The first battery that's designed to disappear."

### Market Opportunity (Slide 5):
> "This isn't a niche. The EU Battery Regulation creates a **mandated market** of €68 billion by 2030. If you're deploying sensors in Europe after 2026, and you can't retrieve them, you **have** to use something like MykoVolt."

### Competitive Advantage (Slide 6):
> "We have four defensible moats. Technical: Our AI pipeline accelerates R&D by 10x. Manufacturing: Compression molding gives us 100x the speed at 1/10th the cost. Regulatory: We'll be first to market with CE and FDA clearances. Data: Our platform creates network effects that get stronger with each sensor deployed."

### Financials (Slide 11):
> "We're raising €1.75 million to get us from PoC to pilot production. With this, we'll achieve €13.76 million in revenue by 2028, be ready for Series A, and target a €110 million valuation. The math is simple: EU regulations are creating demand, and we're the only ones who can supply it."

### Closing (Slide 15):
> "We're not just building a battery company. We're building the infrastructure for sustainable IoT. The question isn't whether the world needs this — the EU has already decided that it does. The question is: Will you be part of the solution?"
