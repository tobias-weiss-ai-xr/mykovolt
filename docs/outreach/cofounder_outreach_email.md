## Personalization Tips

**Before sending, customize:**

1. **Research the person:**
   - Read their recent papers (last 2-3 years)
   - Check their lab website / Google Scholar
   - Note any relevant projects (MFCs, fungal biology, biodegradable materials, electrochemistry)

2. **Personalize the opening:**
   - Mention a specific paper, project, or talk
   - Explain why their expertise is uniquely relevant
   - Show you've done your homework

3. **Adjust for background:**
   - **Academic researcher:** Emphasize publications, IP, lab autonomy
   - **Industry professional:** Emphasize commercial potential, market size, exit scenarios
   - **PhD student/postdoc:** Emphasize career growth, equity, building from ground up

4. **Add mutual connections (if any):**
   - "We both know [Name] from [context]"
   - "[Name] suggested I reach out"
